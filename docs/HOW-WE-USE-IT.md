# How Aug's Dogs works for the group

The idea: **one standing date, one random spot, zero planning effort.** The app does the
deciding so nobody has to organize. Whoever shows up, shows up.

## The weekly rhythm

| When | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon–Tue** | Someone opens the app and taps **Spin the wheel**. The pick is locked in for everyone (one walk per week). | Rotate the "spinner" job, or whoever's first. |
| **Right after the spin** | **Share invite** → paste into the group chat. It has the place, time, directions and the link. | The spinner |
| **During the week** | People tap **I'm in!** so everyone can see the headcount and whose dogs are coming. Edit the meeting spot if the lot is tricky ("meet at the Plain Rd lot"). | Everyone |
| **Thu–Fri** | Quick look at the card for warnings (blaze-orange season, beach bans, Block Island ferry). If the pick doesn't work (weather, a dog is sick, too far for most), hit **🎲 Re-roll**. Re-roll early, not the morning of. | Anyone |
| **Sunday 2 PM** | Walk. | Whoever can make it |
| **Sunday evening** | Everyone drops photos into that walk's album. The pin turns green on the map. | Everyone |

## Small rules that keep it fun

- **No vetoes, only re-rolls.** One re-roll per week is a good norm; more and it stops being random.
- **Use the filters when they matter.** "Easy walks only" when there's an old or tiny dog or a puppy coming. "★ Favourites only" for a guaranteed-good week. Turn off far regions in the winter, and turn Block Island on for a summer adventure day.
- **The map is the scrapbook.** Over a year the map fills with green pins, each with photos. That's the payoff, and a good reason for people to keep adding photos.
- **Grow the hat together.** Anyone who knows a great spot adds it with **+ Add a spot**, and it can come up for everyone from then on.
- **Purple pins are for humans-only outings.** Sachuest, Norman Bird Sanctuary, Long Pond Woods and the rest are stunning but ban dogs. Good for a "leave the dogs home" day, never for the weekly walk.

## Ideas once it's a habit

- A pinned message in the group chat with the link and "spin on Monday".
- A yearly "Paw Awards" from the map: most walks attended, best photo, muddiest dog.
- Seasonal traditions: a Block Island day in June, an off-season beach run in January, Stepstone Falls after a big rain.
- If the group grows: a second weekday evening walk on short, easy spots near Providence.

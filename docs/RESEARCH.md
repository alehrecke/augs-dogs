## Connecticut & Massachusetts (added 2026-10-19)

21 dog walks within ~47 straight-line miles of Providence, plus Caratunk (Audubon RI, Seekonk) and
Allens Pond (Mass Audubon) in the no-dogs layer.
- **CT state parks and forests:** dogs on a leash of 7 ft or less. No dogs in buildings or swimming areas, and none on the beaches at Harkness, Rocky Neck or Silver Sands year-round ([CT DEEP policies](https://portal.ct.gov/DEEP/State-Parks/Policies---CT-State-Parks-and-Forests)). No hunting on Sundays in CT.
- **MA DCR parks:** leashed dogs allowed except at listed places (Walden, Moore SP, Boston Harbor Islands; [Dogs in DCR parks](https://www.mass.gov/guides/dogs-in-dcr-parks)). Purgatory Chasm and Freetown SF confirm dogs on their own pages.
- **Mass Audubon:** "dog walking ... prohibited" at its sanctuaries ([Allens Pond](https://www.massaudubon.org/places-to-explore/wildlife-sanctuaries/allens-pond)).
- **Bluff Point:** sources disagree on dogs on the beach Apr–Sep, so the app tip says to stick to the trails in summer.

## Rules worth knowing

- State parks: leash ≤ 6 ft. State management areas: leash ≤ 25 ft, and no loose dogs Mar 15 – Aug 15 ([RI regs](https://rules.sos.ri.gov/regulations/Part/250-100-00-9)).
- State swimming beaches: no dogs Apr 1 – Sep 30. Block Island beaches allow leashed dogs all year.
- Blaze orange in management areas during hunting seasons: roughly the second Saturday in Sept to the end of Feb, and the third Saturday in Apr to May 31. It's 200 sq in, or 500 sq in during December shotgun deer season. Block Island trailheads have loaner vests.
- Roger Williams Park: leashed dogs in non-recreational areas only.
- Ninigret NWR: leashed dogs are OK on the Kettle Pond visitor-center trails (except Toupoysett Pond Trail), **not** on the Salt Pond unit.
- Lawton Farm (Scituate): no dogs Apr 1 – Aug 31 (nesting).
- Aquidneck Land Trust: dogs on leash everywhere except Oakland Forest.

## No-dogs places: enforcement findings

Researched because Alex asked whether anyone has actually been caught.

- **National wildlife refuges (Sachuest, Trustom, Ninigret Salt Pond):** federal land. Pets are banned under 50 CFR 26.21(b). Refuge officers can write a federal citation (27.51), and the fine comes from the district court's collateral schedule. Animals can also be impounded (50 CFR 28.43). The RI refuge complex manager told RI Monthly that off-leash dogs are his #1 visitor complaint. **No published news of a specific RI dog ticket was found**, but this is the one category with real federal teeth.
- **Audubon refuges (15):** private. Dogs are banned at all of them, posted at trailheads, and the reasons are given (wildlife sees dogs as predators; scent lingers). No fines or tickets found. Enforcement is staff and other visitors asking you to leave.
- **Norman Bird Sanctuary:** staffed entrance with an admission fee. Dogs are turned away at the gate.
- **TNC Whitehead Preserve, land-trust no-dog parcels:** posted owner rules. No enforcement records found.

Bottom line for the group: these are great humans-only walks. Bringing dogs risks a federal
citation at the refuges and being turned away or confronted elsewhere. The bans exist mostly for
ground-nesting and migrating birds.
